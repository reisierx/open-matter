# Decisions

- **Name.** The convention is **open-matter**. PDF is the first profile, not the product. Domain `open-matter.org`. Repo `reisierx/open-matter`.
- **Spec id.** Writers use `open-matter/0.1` and `open-matter.yaml`. Readers still accept `pdf-frontmatter/0.1` and `agent-frontmatter.yaml`.
- **Headline.** Stop making AI re-read the same file. The card is **exhaust**: written on a read someone was already paying for. Not an investment you pre-pay hoping for re-reads.
- **What we are not.** Not a living-document write/patch layer. Not a markdown profile for mAInd. Not prepaid batch enrichment of cold archives. mAInd may steal the *idea*; it is not a workstream here. Live use is the Claude plugin on real PDFs.
- **Where the card dies.** Cold archives (power law). Inside one firm a database wins. Counterparty cards cannot be trusted without a re-read that deletes the savings.
- **Where the card lives.** Your files across your tools. Files you yourself issue (schema.org shape).
- **Category copy.** Portable document memory. Portability is the unique claim. The rest is supporting evidence.
- **Value display.** At least two of: multiple, percentage, money. Never a dollar figure below $1. Break-even is read two. Do not sell prepaid fleet carding.
- **Card write.** Envelope stays tiny (`spec`, `title`, hash). The value is `facts: [{fact, page}]` plus typed entities. A number without a page is a writer bug.
- **Evals.** Written from the pages, never the card. Structure + ≥75% of questions must pass. A card that fails is not attached.
- **Nav.** Home, App, Spec.
- **MCP.** `packages/mcp-open-matter`.
- **GitHub.** `reisierx/open-matter`.
- **Packages.** Not on npm/PyPI yet. Do not pretend they are.
- **Analytics.** None.

## Kill criteria (set 14 August 2026)

Clock: **90 days → 12 November 2026.**

- **Pass.** People who are not reisierx write cards weekly through the plugin or `/app`, **and** at least one unsolicited integration or serious inbound. We found something.
- **Miss.** Stop treating this as a product. Keep the tooling for our own PDFs. No more homepage, no more formats, no more GTM. Archive with pride.

Until then: PDF + plugin + exhaust positioning. Single-player value has to carry this alone. The convention is option value.
